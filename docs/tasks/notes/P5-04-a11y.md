## 2. Findings and fixes

- **F1 — `aria-label` on elements whose role takes no name** (axe `aria-prohibited-attr`, serious): the connection state and the "summary" counts (wide bar and phone menu), the card facts (`dd`: trust level, cost so far, wall clock, tool calls), the parked card's "offers", the Steward's "current activity", the runtime and access-token blocks in settings, a status report in a thread, a withdrawn question, a history row's agents. Fixed with a role that takes a name: `status` (connection), `group` (summaries, offers, activity, runtime, token, report, withdrawn, agents), `definition` on the `dd`s. The managed-agents card's question badge lost its label instead: "open questions" was hiding the count it labelled ("1 open question" is now read as written).
- **F2 — no skip link.** "Skip to main content" is the first stop of the top bar (shown only while focused); it puts the focus on the main area's `h1` (the selected agent's name, or the page title).
- **F3 — a page change left the focus behind.** Going to `/settings`, `/history` or back to the panel now puts the focus on the new page's `h1` when it was lost or in the top bar (`useRouteFocus`). The "N waiting for you" button keeps its own behaviour (it focuses the inbox). Changes P3-12's "a menu link returns the focus to the menu button" for links to another page (the test was updated).
- **F4 — navigation: Tab only.** Arrow keys added, a light ARIA tree pattern (**chosen over a full `role="tree"` with roving tabindex**, so rows keep their link role, `aria-current`, middle-click and copy-link, and Tab still reaches every link, "+", and "⋯"): Down / Up / Home / End between rows (from the link, "+" or "⋯" of a row); Right opens a closed row, then enters its first nested row; Left closes an open row, then goes to the row it is nested in. A row with no agent yet focuses its first button.
- **F5 — lists of cards: Tab only.** Down / Up / Home / End move between the inbox's cards, the managed-agents cards (and the "Reports to" links) and the history rows (`listKeys`): the card itself gets `tabindex="-1"` and the focus, so a screen reader reads its name; from a button inside a card the arrows go to the neighbouring card. Fields and widgets that use the arrows (inputs, selects, textareas, sliders, the resize handle) keep them.
- **F6 — a card that went away dropped the focus** (answered here or elsewhere, approved, a stale banner dismissed, the Steward's clarification box after the answer). `useFocusRescue` (a `MutationObserver` on the inbox, the managed agents, the navigation and the main area) gives the focus to the item now at the same place, else the last, else the list's heading or region. A user who moved the focus away on purpose is left alone.
- **F7 — confirmations.** "Stop / Cancel / Close / Archive now" opened an inline confirmation while the focus stayed on the destructive button or was lost (an action from the closed "More" menu). The confirmation now takes the focus on "Keep" (the safe answer), Escape keeps, and closing gives the focus back to what opened it, else "More", else the first button near it.
- **F8 — "More" said `aria-haspopup="true"`** (a menu) for a group of buttons. Now a disclosure: `aria-expanded` + `aria-controls`.
- **F9 — notifications popover.** Opening now moves the focus into the dialog (its first control that can take focus, else the dialog); Escape closes it and gives the focus back to its button.
- **F10 — a row's "⋯"** now names the actions group it opens (`aria-controls`).
- **F11 — "Edit" on a card** now says whether its form is open (`aria-expanded`); the card's and the controls' edit forms put the focus on their first field when they open.
- **F12 — history inspector.** "Close" dropped the focus; Escape did nothing. Closing (button or Escape, not while typing in the search) now gives the focus back to the "Inspect" button or link that opened it, else the page's heading. Down / Up / Home / End move between the history rows (F5).
- **F13 — wizard steps.** Next / Back removed the pressed button; the focus now goes to the new step's title.
- **F14 — legend toggle.** "Legend" and "Hide" are different buttons; the focus now follows across.
- **F15 — replay exit.** "Exit replay" (or Escape) dropped the focus; it now goes back to what opened the replay ("Replay history" in a row's "⋯", "Replay" in the history), else the main area's heading.
- **F16 — a side-column "Hide"** (managed agents, inbox) dropped the focus; it now goes to the top bar's "Show …" button (the menu button on a phone).
- **F17 — the "waiting for you" count changed silently** for a screen reader. A visually hidden polite live region says "New in the inbox: N waiting for you." / "N waiting for you." / "Nothing waits for you any more." once per change, never on load. Existing live regions kept: the chat and transcript logs, "N new" replies, the replay's status.
- **F18 — reduced motion.** The beacon pulse already stopped; nothing else in the panel animates today, but smooth scrolling to the inbox and to a revealed card did not follow the preference. One rule now turns off every animation and transition under `.pnl` with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` (so future ones are covered), each stylesheet that animates keeps its own block (labels, legend, beacons' outline, the beacon dot), and `scrollBehavior()` jumps instead of scrolling smoothly.
- **F19 — contrast (spot check of the tokens).** The light theme's beacon was white 11 px bold text on amber `#d9820f`: 2.9:1, under AA. Its ink is now the dark `#1d1300` (6.2:1); the amber itself is unchanged. The skip link uses the page background on the blue (5.7:1 dark, 4.9:1 light). The focus ring (`--pnl-here`) is 5.7:1 / 5.4:1 / 4.9:1 against the dark background, surface and raised surface, 4.9:1 / 5.6:1 in light: above the 3:1 needed for a focus indicator. Muted text is 5.9:1 (dark) and 5.2:1 (light).

## 3. Deferred

1. **A "?" keyboard-shortcuts help** (optional in the task): not built. The shortcuts are listed in §5; a small help popover can come with the PWA work.
2. **Colour contrast across every component** is not automated: jsdom has no layout engine, so axe's `color-contrast` rule is off. A run of axe in a real browser (Playwright + `@axe-core/playwright`, or axe DevTools by hand) over the same views, in dark and light, is the follow-up.
3. **A real screen-reader pass** (VoiceOver on macOS and iOS) has not been done; the ARIA was checked by axe and by reading.
4. **The transcript and chat logs are live regions** (`role="log"`): a Steward printing long tool output may be chatty for a screen reader. Consider `aria-live="off"` on the Steward's transcript with a polite "N new lines" summary.
5. **The navigation landmark is named "navigation"**, read as "navigation navigation". Renaming it ("work") touches many tests that query it by that name; low value, left.
6. **The satellite hover card is pointer-only.** The same facts (role, work, state, activity, body) are in the navigation, the dock bar and the labels, so nothing is lost without a mouse; noted for completeness.
7. **The replay bar is not in the axe run** (it needs a recorded timeline as fixture); its ARIA was read: named region, labelled buttons and slider with `aria-valuetext`, `status` / `alert` lines.
8. **After "Yes" on a confirmation** the opener may be disabled while the request runs; the focus then falls back to the list's or main area's heading instead of the opener.
9. **Focus order inside the stacked layout (≤ 1100 px)** follows the DOM (scene, navigation, side column, then the dock); a "skip to the conversation" link for the phone could save Tab presses there.

|---|---|

| Conversation | Enter sends · Shift+Enter: new line |

| Replay | Escape exits (after any open popover or menu) |

## 7. Proposed SPEC text

A new decision for §7 (the next free number), refining §4.2 and §5:

> **Keyboard and screen readers** (2026-10-04, P5-04; §4.2, §5, decisions 62, 67, 71): the panel is usable with the keyboard alone and with a screen reader, with or without the scene. Tab reaches every control in reading order, starting with "Skip to main content"; arrow keys move through the navigation (Right / Left open and close a project's work) and through the lists of cards and history rows, as a shortcut — Tab is never replaced. Whatever opens (a menu, a popover, a confirmation, an edit form, the history inspector) takes the focus, Escape closes it, and the focus goes back to what opened it; a page change puts the focus on the new page's title; when an item the user was on goes away (a question answered, a card decided), the focus moves to the next one. Confirmations start on the safe answer ("Keep"). A change of the "waiting for you" count is announced politely, never on load. With the system's reduced-motion setting nothing in the panel pulses, blinks or slides.

## 8. Review fixes

From the review of the P5-04 batch:

1. **The wide bar's connection text was a live region.** `role="status"` had been added only to carry its `aria-label`, so "connecting / catching up / live" was announced on load and on every reconnect retry. It is now a named `role="img"` ("connection: live"), like the compact bar's dot; no live semantics. Test: `TopBar.test.tsx`.
2. **A replay opened from the history page lost the focus on exit.** The opener was captured after the route change, when the history page (and its "Replay" button) had unmounted: it recorded `document.body`, and exiting with the focus in the bar focused the body. The body (or nothing) now counts as no opener, so the focus goes to the main area's heading. Test: `replay-ui.test.tsx`, "Replay from history, then Escape".
3. **An answered question in the main area sent the focus to the title** even when another question was waiting. The main area's focus rescue now knows its items — the open question cards (inline in the thread, in the Steward view) and the Steward view's clarification box — and gives the focus to the next card, else the main area's title. Test: `keyboard.test.tsx`, three questions answered in turn in the Steward view.
4. **The list arrows could strand an open menu or confirmation.** Down / Up / Home / End from inside a `data-escape-layer` (the "More" menu) or a pending confirmation (`.agc-confirm`) moved the focus to the next card and left it open. The arrows now do nothing there (chosen over closing "More" on focus-out, which would also close it on a click into the scene). Test: `keyboard.test.tsx`. The a11y tests also start the store at the URL's route now: `reset()` keeps the route, so the previous test's route changing after mount made `useRouteFocus` move the focus mid-test (it showed as a flaky notifications-popover test).
5. **The axe checks of the wizard and the Steward view** could run on a loading state; they now wait for the wizard's title and the Steward view's open questions and answer box.
6. **Two `role="group"`s carried only a name**: the access token's `<code>` (its visible "Token" label already names it: role and label dropped) and the Steward's one-line activity `<p>` (role and label dropped; a visually hidden "Current activity: " prefix keeps what the name said). axe stays clean.

Focus rules, as now written in TECHNOLOGY §2.1: popovers, confirmations and edit forms take the focus; disclosures (More, ⋯) keep it on their toggle, Tab enters them; when a focused item goes away the focus goes to the next card, else the list's heading or, in the main area, its title.
